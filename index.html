How to Use the ROAS & Break-Even Simulator

Mastering unit economics protects your ecommerce business from unprofitable ad scaling. Follow these simple steps:

1

Enter True Product Costs

Fill in the retail price, supplier product cost (COGS), shipping fee, and your payment processor rate (typically 2.9% + $0.30 for Shopify Payments or Stripe).

2

Review Your Break-Even Threshold

The simulator automatically calculates your Break-Even ROAS and Max CPA. If your ads perform below this ROAS, each order loses money.

3

Simulate Target Ad Returns

Slide the Target ROAS bar to inspect your net profit margin and total campaign income across 100 or more orders. Use Copy Financial Breakdown to share reports with your team.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Break-Even ROAS and how is it calculated?

Break-Even ROAS represents the ad return where you make $0 profit after all costs. It is computed as Sale Price / (Sale Price - COGS - Shipping - Gateway Fee).

What is the difference between ROAS and ROI?

ROAS (Return On Ad Spend) measures gross revenue divided by ad spend. ROI (Return On Investment) measures your final net profit divided by all combined business costs.

Why do small changes in ROAS drastically affect net profit?

Because product costs and payment fees remain fixed per unit, every increase in ROAS goes straight to your bottom line, causing exponential changes in net cash flow.

Does this simulator work completely offline?

Yes. All logic is self-contained in this single HTML file and runs fully offline without an internet connection.
